
# Farm Idle Game

Grow, harvest, preserve, and profit—build your farming empire one plot at a time!

Farm Idle Game is a relaxing and strategic idle clicker where you plant, harvest, and process a variety of crops into preserved goods. Unlock powerful upgrades, weather the changing seasons, keep bees, join a guild, expand your farm, and prestige for bigger rewards. Balance active canning gameplay with passive farming automation to maximize your profits!

The game features growing, canning and beekeeping systems, guilds, two seasonal events with their own farms and currencies, 44 achievements, persistent progress that keeps going while you're away, a knowledge system and Legacy, a prestige built on the guilds, farm expansion, dynamic weather events, in-game help pages, automation throughout, and extensive accessibility features. Nearly every image is cozy 32×32 pixel art.

## Gameplay Overview

### 🌱 Growing System
- **Simultaneous Growth:** All unlocked crops grow at the same time, each with its own growth rate and favorite seasons
- **10 Vegetables:** Radish, Lettuce, Green Beans, Zucchini, Cucumbers, Tomatoes, Peppers, Carrots, Broccoli and Onions, unlocked in order with experience
- **10 Fruits:** Strawberry through Watermelon, opened by the Growers Guild's Fruit Cultivation upgrade and unlocked with 50,000 to about 60 million experience
- **Visual Progress:** Real-time progress bars show growth percentages and estimated days to maturity
- **Harvesting:** Click to collect grown crops or buy an Auto Harvester for each crop
- **Strategic Selling:** Choose which crops to sell or stockpile with each crop's Sell or Hold button
- **Reward Balance:** Manual harvesting gives full experience and knowledge; auto-harvesting gives half, except for the Engineers, whose machines get full rewards

### 🥫 Canning System (Unlocks at Farm Tier 3)
- **Recipe Processing:** Turn raw crops into preserved goods worth more than their ingredients
- **42 Recipes:** Simple, complex and gourmet recipes; 16 need Golden Honey, 2 jar honey alone once you buy Honey Jars, 10 Cellar recipes belong to the Preservers, and new ones unlock with canning experience
- **The Kitchen:** The pantry shows your crops and honey, the stove shows every batch cooking, and the recipe book shows each recipe's ingredients, value and time
- **Stove Slots:** Run up to 16 batches at once (upgradeable with Batch Canning)
- **Automation:** The Canner starts a batch every 10 seconds, using the first recipe in your sort order that your stash covers
- **The Cannery:** Engineers run a factory floor instead, where each machine makes the recipe you set
- **Enhanced Rewards:** Earn money, experience, AND knowledge from completed recipes; Canner batches give less knowledge
- **Resource Management:** Balance selling fresh crops against processing them

### 🐝 Bee System (Unlocks at Farm Tier 4)
- **Bee Hives:** Start with 2 hives and buy more with honey, up to 30
- **Honey:** Each hive makes a 15 lb batch every 132 days and keeps stockpiling until you collect; bees rest in winter unless you buy Winter Hardiness
- **Golden Honey:** Each batch has a 1% base chance to be golden; used in premium recipes
- **Pollination:** Every hive adds +0.5% crop yield, raised further by Meadow Magic and Flower Power
- **Beekeeper Assistant:** Hire him at 4 hives for 750 honey; he collects every batch as it's made and speeds up the bees
- **Honey Jars:** For 1,000 honey, the Kitchen jars honey and Golden Honey on their own
- **Honey Recipes:** Honey unlocks new canning recipes

### 🛡️ Guilds (Unlock at Farm Tier 5)
- **Four Guilds:** Growers, Preservers, Engineers and Merchants, each with its own philosophy, bonuses and trade-offs
- **Guild Tokens:** Before you commit, earn tokens from manual harvests (+1 each), canned goods (+1 per 5), batches of honey (+2 per batch) and auto harvests (+1 per 10)
- **Sampling:** Try up to two starter upgrades from each guild before you choose; unspent tokens don't carry over when you join, and a guild's currency is earned only as a member
- **Growers Guild:** Commit to earn Sigils from manual harvests and spend them on 8 guild upgrades, including Fruit Cultivation
  - **Bonuses:** Extra crops on manual harvests and stronger bee pollination
  - **Trade-offs:** Auto harvesters run 25% slower and canning pays 15% less
- **Ritual Harvest:** With Ritual Circles, spend 5 Sigils to harvest every ready crop at once
- **Preservers Guild:** Commit to earn Wax Seals from batches you start and spend them on 10 guild upgrades, including Cellar Recipes and the Pressure Canner
  - **Bonuses:** Jars sell for 20% more, Recipe Mastery, Prize Jars and Rush Batch
  - **Trade-offs:** Raw crops sell for 10% less, and Dunkin the Glutton raids the pantry
- **Engineers Guild:** Commit to earn Gears from auto harvests, factory floor batches and Stanley, and spend them on 10 guild upgrades, including Bulk Purchasing and Night Shift
  - **Bonuses:** Every crop gets a faster Auto Harvester with full rewards, batches run 30% faster, and the Kitchen becomes the Cannery
  - **Trade-offs:** No manual harvests or canning, crops sell for 15% less and jars for 25% less, and the bees make 25% less honey
- **Tune-Up:** Click Stanley the cat when he visits, and every machine runs twice as fast for a minute
- **Merchants Coming:** The Merchants can be previewed and sampled now; joining them comes in a future update
- **Legacy:** From Farm Tier 6, end your Legacy to start the farm over and keep half your guild levels, one unlock and your special achievements; then choose a guild again

### 🏆 Progression Systems
- **Experience Unlocking:** Gain experience from harvesting to unlock crops in sequence
- **Farm Expansion:** Buy a larger farm for more max plots (+1 per 100 experience, up to double); crops and upgrades reset, but you keep your knowledge and leftover money
- **Prestige/Knowledge:** Earn knowledge points from harvesting and canning to buy upgrades
- **Farm Tiers:** Each tier adds knowledge to every harvest, starts you with more experience, and opens new systems: Canning at Tier 3, Bees at Tier 4, Guilds at Tier 5

### Upgrade Details

#### Per-Crop Upgrades (Growing Tab)

- **Fertilizer:** Increases growth rate by +5% per level for the selected crop
- **Better Seeds (Knowledge):** Raises the selected crop's sale price by 25% per level (50% with Heirloom Seeds)
- **Additional Plot:** Adds +1 crop per harvest for the selected crop, using one of your farm's plots
- **Auto Harvester:** Harvests the selected crop on a timer, every 50 seconds at first; click it to pause or resume
- **Harvester Speed:** Makes the Auto Harvester 5% faster per level
- **Sell Control:** Toggle 💰 Sell / 🚫 Hold to control which crops the Sell button and the Merchant sell

#### Global Upgrades

- **Farmer's Almanac:** Increases knowledge gain by +10% per level
- **Irrigation:** Provides a constant +15% growth rate bonus AND negates drought penalties ($750 + 75 Knowledge)
- **Merchant:** Sells every crop set to 💰 Sell once every 30 days ($1,000 + 100 Knowledge)
- **Greenhouse:** Removes winter and snow growth penalties for all crops (cost scales with farm size)
- **Heirloom Seeds:** Doubles the effect of Better Seeds (cost scales with the highest crop you've unlocked); Engineers know it as GMO Seeds

#### Auto-Purchaser System

- **Assistant:** Auto-purchases Fertilizer upgrades every 7 days using money
- **Cultivator:** Auto-purchases Better Seeds upgrades every 7 days using knowledge
- **Surveyor:** Auto-purchases Additional Plot upgrades every 7 days using money (respects limits)
- **Mechanic:** Auto-purchases Harvester Speed upgrades every 7 days using money
- **Smart Management:** Auto-purchasers disable when unable to purchase and show helpful tooltips
- **Unified Timer:** All auto-purchasers share a single 7-day cycle with visual progress bar

#### Canning Upgrades (Canning Tab)

- **Quick Hands:** Reduces canning time by 5% per level
- **Family Recipe:** Increases the sale price of canned goods by 10% per level
- **Heirloom Touch:** Chance to get bonus canned goods from a batch
- **Batch Canning:** Adds processing slots to run more recipes at once
- **Canner (Automation):** Starts canning batches automatically every 10 seconds

#### Bee Upgrades (Bees Tab)

- **Production:** Busy Bees, Super Frames, Nectar Efficiency and Swift Gatherers make more honey, faster
- **Golden Honey:** Royal Jelly, Queen's Blessing and Golden Touch raise the Golden Honey chance
- **Crop Yield:** Meadow Magic and Flower Power raise the pollination bonus for your crops
- **Winter Hardiness:** Bees keep producing honey through the winter
- **Beekeeper Assistant:** Collects honey from ready hives automatically
- **Honey Jars:** Opens the Kitchen's jars of honey and Golden Honey

## Features

### 🌦️ Environmental Systems
- **Four Dynamic Seasons:** Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter - each lasting ~90 days
- **Seasonal Crop Bonuses:** Each vegetable grows faster in its favorite seasons
- **Winter:** Crops grow at 10% speed unless you own a Greenhouse
- **Weather Events:** Clear, Rain (+20% growth), Storm (+10%), Drought (-50%), Heatwave, Snow
  - **Heatwave:** -30% in Summer; in Spring and Fall, summer crops grow 10% faster and the rest 30% slower; +20% in Winter
  - **Snow:** Stops growth unless you own a Greenhouse
- **Environmental Protection:** 
  - **Irrigation:** +15% growth bonus in ALL conditions + drought protection ($750 + 75 Knowledge)
  - **Greenhouse:** Removes Winter (-90%) and Snow (-100%) penalties (scales with farm size)

### 🏅 Achievements
- **44 Achievements:** Across farming, wealth, progression, canning, bees, Dunkin's and Stanley's visits, the factory floor, Legacies and both seasonal events
- **Rewards:** Money, knowledge, Candy Corn, Archie outfits, a cocoa recipe and permanent crop bonuses
- **Permanent Bonuses:** Frost Fertilizer (+5% winter crop yield) and Harvest Moon (+5% fall crop yield)

- **Auto-Save System:** Progress saves to localStorage automatically every 30 seconds
- **Export/Import:** Timestamped save files for backup and sharing

- **Backward Compatibility:** Seamless migration when new features are added
- **Year & Day Tracking:** Long-term progression display showing "Year: X | Day: Y"

## 🎮 New Player Guide

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with Radish and focus on Fertilizer upgrades for faster growth
2. **Use the Help System**: Click the "Info" button in the header for guides on each system
3. **Manual vs Auto**: Manual harvesting gives full experience and knowledge; auto gives half - balance active play with idle progression
4. **Expand Your Farm**: Larger farms open Canning at Farm Tier 3, Bees at Tier 4 and Guilds at Tier 5
5. **Plot Management**: Watch plot warnings! Don't fill all plots with Additional Plots or you can't unlock new crops
6. **Weather Awareness**: Check the weather icon and plan around seasonal bonuses for your crops
7. **Sell Strategy**: Set valuable crops to Hold on their beds to stockpile them for canning, and leave cheaper ones on Sell
8. **Upgrade Timing**: Major upgrades like Greenhouse scale with farm size - plan purchases carefully
9. **Canning Strategy**: Once unlocked, balance selling raw crops vs. processing them for higher profits
10. **Seasonal Events**: The Pumpkin Rush runs Sep 22 through Thanksgiving and the Christmas Tree Shop Nov 1-Dec 25, each with its own farm and currency
11. **Archie Hunting**: Keep an eye out for Archie - clicking him gives bonus money and builds streaks!
12. **Tooltips**: Hover over buttons and UI elements for detailed information about mechanics and formulas
13. **Pick a Guild**: At Farm Tier 5, sample a few upgrades, then join the Growers for farming, the Preservers for canning or the Engineers for machines that run the farm
14. **Legacy**: At Farm Tier 6, end your Legacy from the Guilds tab to start over with half your guild levels and one unlock kept
15. **Save Management**: Use the Settings panel to export saves regularly as backups

## 🔧 For Developers

- **React + TypeScript**: Modern React 19 with full TypeScript support and strict mode
- **Vite Build System**: Fast development and optimized production builds (about 640 KB of JavaScript, 186 KB gzipped)
- **Game Clock**: one `GameClock` steps every system once per second, in a fixed order, so React renders once per step (`useGameTick` subscribes)
- **Catch-Up**: `useOfflineProgress` replays time away and hidden-tab time, capped at 8 hours (`MAX_OFFLINE_MS`); canning catches up through `utils/canningSimulation.ts`
- **State Management**: Centralized game state with localStorage persistence and ref-based access patterns
- **Component Architecture**: Modular BaseTab system with reusable upgrade panels; both seasonal events share `EventUpgradesPanel` and `utils/eventUpgrades.ts`
- **Error Handling**: A top-level error boundary shows the error and a Retry button instead of a blank page
- **Test Suite**: Comprehensive tests with Vitest, Testing Library, and coverage reporting
- **Build Commands**:
  - `npm run dev` - Start development server
  - `npm run build` - Production build with type checking; leaves images the game never loads, like Archie's full-size originals, out of dist
  - `npm run test` - Run test suite in watch mode
  - `npm run coverage` - Generate test coverage report
  - `npm run lint` - Run ESLint checks
  - `npm run typecheck` - Type-check the app, the tests and the Vite config
  - `npm run check` - Type check, lint and run all tests, the same checks CI runs
- **Commit checks**: `npm install` points git at `.githooks/`, whose pre-commit hook runs `npm run check`. A failing check stops the commit. GitHub Actions runs the same checks plus a production build on every push to `main` and on pull requests.
